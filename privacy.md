# Kismet for iPhone and Apple TV: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026.

This policy covers the Kismet app for iPhone and Apple TV, published by Defiant Labs.

## What we collect

Nothing. Defiant Labs doesn't collect, receive or store any data from the app. It has no account, analytics, advertising or backend service of ours.

## What stays on your device

- **Connection settings:** your server address, user name and any location you enter for your server are stored on the device. Your API token or password is stored in the device's Keychain and isn't synced to other devices.
- **Server data:** what the app reads from your Kismet server is shown while the app runs and isn't saved to the device.

The app doesn't use your device's location. Deleting the app removes its settings, though iOS and tvOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted.

## Who the app talks to

- **Your Kismet server:** the app connects only to the server you configure, and sends it your login. That server is yours; we have no access to it or to what it records.
- **Apple Maps:** the aircraft map uses Apple's MapKit, so map requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Apple may also give us anonymous App Store and crash statistics if you've agreed to share them with developers. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

## Data from your server

A Kismet server can record identifiers of nearby devices, such as network names and hardware addresses. You control that server and its data, and you're responsible for using it lawfully.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page, with a new effective date.
